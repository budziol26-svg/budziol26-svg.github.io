# Privacy Policy — HA AI Agent

Last updated: October 29, 2026

HA AI Agent is a personal, non-commercial application used by its owner to monitor and diagnose a private Home Assistant installation.

## Information processed

The application may process Home Assistant entity identifiers, device states, automation configuration, and diagnostic reports.

## Google Drive access

Google Drive OAuth is used to store and retrieve diagnostic reports and task files in a folder controlled by the owner. The application is not intended to access unrelated files.

## Data storage and sharing

Diagnostic data is stored locally on the owner's Raspberry Pi and in the owner's configured Google Drive folder. It is not published on this website, sold, or used for advertising.

## Security

OAuth credentials and Home Assistant access tokens are intended to be stored locally and are not published in this repository.

## Data control

The owner can delete diagnostic files from the Raspberry Pi and Google Drive and revoke the application's Google access through their Google Account settings.
